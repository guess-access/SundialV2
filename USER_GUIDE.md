# Sundial Solstice — User & Staff Guide

A practical guide to signing in, recording time, reviewing attendance, and getting help in Sundial Solstice.

## Overview

Sundial Solstice brings timekeeping, schedules, attendance, time-off requests, and payroll-hour submissions together. Available tabs and actions depend on your account role. Staff see their own records; team leads and admins may have additional team tools and routed requests.

Times are shown in US Eastern time (EST/EDT) in a 12-hour clock. Use the theme control to switch between light and dark display settings.

## For users / staff

After signing in, use the navigation tabs to move between **Clock**, **My timesheet**, **Attendance** (shown as **Schedules** for authorized roles), and **My Info**. Your name/profile at the top opens My Info. Announcements or banners may appear below the navigation.

Staff can record their own clock activity, view their own timesheet and attendance, update permitted profile details, and submit requests. Staff cannot directly rewrite or delete an already recorded shift, export timesheets or punch logs, or manage accounts. Submit a correction request if a punch needs fixing.

## Sign in and clock in/out

1. Open the Sundial Solstice site and enter the username and passcode supplied by your administrator.
2. On the **Clock** tab, check the displayed date, Eastern time, and schedule for the day.
3. Select **Clock in** when you begin work.
4. Use the relevant status button to start or end a break, lunch, or offline period. Check your current status and today's punch log to see what is in progress and what has been recorded.
5. Select **Clock out** when your shift ends. Confirm your punches in **Today**, then review the completed day in **My timesheet**.

If you cannot clock in because power or internet is unavailable, submit a **Report power / internet outage** request when you can. Avoid creating a duplicate shift for a day that already has recorded hours.

## Breaks, lunch, and offline time rules

**Breaks, Offline Hours & Lunch are considered unpaid.** All recorded break, lunch, and offline/outage minutes are deducted from the elapsed shift duration when calculating hours rendered.

`hours rendered = max(0, clock-out − clock-in − all unpaid break/lunch/offline minutes)`

For example, a 9-hour shift with 30 minutes of breaks, 1 hour of lunch, and 15 minutes offline renders 7 hours 15 minutes.

Start and end each pause using its matching Clock action. Confirm that it has ended; an open pause continues to accrue until closed. Break, lunch, and offline minutes appear in their own timesheet columns and attendance summaries. Break records remain visible even though their time is unpaid.

## My timesheet and status

1. Open **My timesheet**.
2. Choose **Weekly** or **Pay period**, or set the **From** and **To** dates. **This week** resets the range.
3. Review each date's status, clock-in/out times, pause durations, worked time, and any paid leave.
4. Status examples include **On time**, **Late**, **Early logout**, **Absent**, **Still clocked in**, **Rest day**, **Flexible hours**, and **On leave**.
5. On a day tile in **Attendance**, open the day's details to review logs and totals. Staff see only their own attendance.

A day with no shift may show dashes rather than punch times. Absence status is based on the schedule and clock-in window; a shift that has not started yet is not immediately marked absent.

## Requests and approvals

Use the action offered on the relevant screen:

- **Request edit** on a shift to ask for a correction. Add a clear note and provide corrected clock or pause times as applicable. Staff requests go to the assigned lead/admin review queue; staff do not directly alter an existing shift.
- **Request schedule change** on the Clock tab to ask for a schedule update. Include the reason and requested weekly hours.
- File time off from a day in the Attendance view. Options include sick leave, paid time off, and unpaid time off. Sick leave requires a PNG, JPEG, or PDF document. Mondays are marked as Critical Working Days and require an approver override.
- File an outage report from the Clock tab if a power/internet problem prevented clocking in. For outages during a shift, use **Offline** so those minutes are recorded and deducted.

If your role provides the Team tab, check **Team → Requests** for items waiting for review. Leads review requests routed to them; admins manage the wider team queue. Approvers can approve or decline requests, and decisions are recorded in the change history. Follow up with your lead or administrator if a request remains pending or if a recorded result needs clarification.

### Before submitting hours

- Check that clock-in and clock-out times are present and correct.
- Confirm every break, lunch, and offline period is closed and recorded accurately.
- Review the worked total and status for each date in the selected range.
- Submit a correction request promptly if anything is wrong; include the date and the times that should be reviewed.
